## Lesson goal

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to use the Present Simple for habits, routines, facts, states and repeated events; form positive, negative and question structures; apply third-person singular spelling; and distinguish it from the Present Continuous.

## 1. What Is It?

The Present Simple presents something as generally true, normal, regular or repeated. It does not usually focus on an action happening at this exact moment.

- I walk to work every morning.
- Water boils at 100°C.
- She works in a hospital.
- They live near the station.

Think of it as the tense for what is normally true across time.

## 2. When Do We Use It?

### Habits and routines

- I check my email after breakfast.
- She goes to the gym three times a week.
- We usually eat dinner at seven.

### Facts and general truths

- The Earth moves around the Sun.
- Plants need water.
- Madrid is the capital of Spain.

### Permanent or long-term situations

- He lives in Valencia.
- I work for an international company.
- They own a small business.

### States, feelings and opinions

- I know the answer.
- She likes classical music.
- We believe you.
- He needs some help.

### Timetables and fixed schedules

- The train leaves at 7:30 tomorrow.
- The lesson starts at nine.
- The shop closes at six.

### Instructions, demonstrations and commentary

- First, you add the flour.
- You turn left at the traffic lights.
- Smith passes the ball and Jones scores.

Common time expressions include **always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, on Mondays** and **once a week**. They are useful clues, but meaning—not a single keyword—chooses the tense.

## 3. Structure / Formula

### Positive

> **Subject + base verb**  
> Add **-s/-es** with **he, she, it**.

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I / You / We / They | work | They work here. |
| He / She / It | works | She works here. |

### Third-person singular spelling

| Pattern | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Most verbs | add **-s** | work → works; read → reads |
| Verbs ending in **-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o** | add **-es** | watch → watches; go → goes |
| Consonant + **y** | change **y** to **ies** | study → studies; carry → carries |
| Vowel + **y** | add **-s** | play → plays; enjoy → enjoys |
| Irregular | learn separately | have → has; be → is |

The verb **be** is different:

> I **am** · You/We/They **are** · He/She/It **is**

## 4. Positive Sentences

- I work from home on Fridays.
- You speak English clearly.
- He drives to work.
- She teaches mathematics.
- The machine makes a strange noise.
- We study together.
- They play football after work.

Do not add **-s** to verbs after **I, you, we** or **they**.

## 5. Negative Sentences

> **Subject + do/does + not + base verb**

- I do not work on Sundays.
- You don’t need an appointment.
- He does not live here.
- She doesn’t drive to work.
- We don’t agree.
- They don’t speak French.

Use **doesn’t** with **he, she, it**. The main verb returns to its base form:

- Correct: She **doesn’t work** here.
- Incorrect: She **doesn’t works** here.

With **be**, do not use do/does:

- I am not tired.
- He isn’t at home.
- They aren’t ready.

## 6. Questions

### Yes/No questions

> **Do/Does + subject + base verb?**

- Do you work here?
- Does she speak Spanish?
- Do they live nearby?

Short answers:

- Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
- Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.

### WH-questions

> **WH-word + do/does + subject + base verb?**

- Where do you live?
- What does he do?
- Why does she study English?
- When do they arrive?

When the question word is the subject, do not use do/does:

- Who works here?
- What causes this problem?

Questions with **be**:

- Are you ready?
- Is she at work?
- Where are they?

## 7. Examples in Real Life

### Home

- I make coffee every morning.
- My children walk to school.

### Work and study

- Our office opens at eight.
- Sara manages the sales team.
- I attend an English class on Tuesdays.

### Preferences and states

- He loves Italian food.
- I understand the problem.
- This bag belongs to Maya.

### Schedules

- The flight departs at 14:20.
- The meeting begins after lunch.

## 8. Common Mistakes

**Wrong:** She work here.  
**Correct:** She works here.  
**Why:** Add **-s/-es** with he, she and it.

**Wrong:** He don’t like coffee.  
**Correct:** He doesn’t like coffee.  
**Why:** Use **doesn’t** with he, she and it.

**Wrong:** She doesn’t works here.  
**Correct:** She doesn’t work here.  
**Why:** Use the base verb after does/doesn’t.

**Wrong:** Does he lives nearby?  
**Correct:** Does he live nearby?  
**Why:** The main verb stays in its base form after **does**.

**Wrong:** I am knowing the answer.  
**Correct:** I know the answer.  
**Why:** **Know** normally describes a state.

**Wrong:** I am agree.  
**Correct:** I agree.  
**Why:** **Agree** is a main verb, not an adjective used with **be**.

## 9. Compare It: Present Simple vs Present Continuous

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|---|---|
| normal, repeated or generally true | current, temporary or developing |
| I work from home. | I’m working from home this week. |
| He drinks coffee every morning. | He’s drinking coffee now. |
| She teaches English. | She is teaching English this semester. |
| The train leaves at seven. | We’re leaving at seven. |

Use the Present Simple for a fixed timetable, but the Present Continuous for a personal arrangement.

## 10. Quick Practice

1. She ________ to work every day. *(walk)*  
   **Answer:** walks

2. Choose: My brother **work / works / is work** in a bank.  
   **Answer:** works

3. Make negative: He plays tennis on Mondays.  
   **Answer:** He doesn’t play tennis on Mondays.

4. Make a question: They live in Barcelona.  
   **Answer:** Do they live in Barcelona?

5. Correct the mistake: She doesn’t likes tea.  
   **Answer:** She doesn’t like tea.

6. Complete: What time ________ the lesson ________? *(start)*  
   **Answer:** does the lesson start

7. Choose the natural form: I **know / am knowing** the answer.  
   **Answer:** know

8. Complete: The train ________ at 6:45 tomorrow. *(leave)*  
   **Answer:** leaves
